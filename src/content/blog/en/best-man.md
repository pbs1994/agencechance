A wedding is one of the most important events in a person's life, and the role of witnesses in this celebration cannot be overstated. A witness at a wedding performs many functions, from officially confirming the marriage union to actively participating in the organization of the celebration. Let's examine in detail all aspects of this responsible role.

## A witness is a person who is officially present at a wedding to attest to the fact that the marriage has taken place. Their role is to sign the marriage certificate after the ceremony, confirming that the couple has legally married.

A witness is a person who officially confirms the fact of a marriage between the groom and the bride. According to Russian legislation, witnesses sign the civil registry book, thereby certifying that the marriage registration ceremony took place in their presence.

Traditionally, two witnesses are chosen: the groom's witness (best man or groomsman) and the bride's witness (maid of honor or bridesmaid). The witness represents the groom's interests, and the witness supports the bride throughout the wedding day.

The role of witnesses goes far beyond simply being present at the ceremony. They become the main assistants to the newlyweds, taking responsibility for organizing many aspects of the celebration.

## International Traditions and Specifics

In different countries, the role of witnesses can vary significantly. In some European countries, witnesses not only sign official documents but also bear legal responsibility for the accuracy of information about the newlyweds. In France, according to official information from the portal [Service-Public.fr](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1248), witnesses must provide documents verifying their identity, and may be held liable for providing false information.

In American tradition, the roles of «Best Man» and «Maid of Honor» are more ceremonial; they organize pre-wedding parties, give speeches, and assist with wedding planning, but they do not have legal obligations. Understanding these differences is important for couples planning an international wedding or a ceremony in another country.

## How to choose a maid of honor and best man

![Wedding Witness. Agence Chance](/media/202508-russkie-na-mavrikii-10-1280.webp)

Choosing witnesses is an important decision for the bride and groom. Typically, newlyweds choose from among their single friends or relatives, although in modern realities, witnesses can be absolutely anyone, including married and divorced individuals.

Traditionally, witnesses are expected to be unmarried, symbolizing their readiness to support the young couple as they begin their family life. However, this requirement is not legally binding.

The groom usually chooses a best man from among his closest friends, and the bride chooses a maid of honor from among her closest female friends. It is important that the chosen individuals are responsible, organized, and willing to actively participate in wedding events.

## Psychological support for newlyweds

One of the most important, yet often underestimated, responsibilities of wedding attendants is providing emotional support to the couple. A wedding day can be stressful for the bride and groom, and as attendants, individuals should be prepared to comfort, encourage, and help manage any anxieties.

The best man/maid of honor must be able to make quick decisions in unforeseen situations, remain calm, and maintain a positive attitude. The wedding celebration should remain in the young couple's memory as a happy day, and much of this depends on how professionally and sensitively the best man/maid of honor fulfills their role. People capable of handling organizational matters should be by the newlyweds' side so that the bride and groom can fully enjoy their special day.

## Duties of a wedding witness

### Preparatory stage

The duties of a witness begin long before the wedding day. A witness must:

-   Help organize a bachelor party for the groom
-   Participate in the selection of a costume and accessories
-   Coordinate the actions of the other groomsmen
-   Prepare a toast for a banquet
-   Brainstorming the details of a bride's ransom

On the eve of the wedding, the best man must ensure that all preparations are completed and that the groom is mentally ready for the solemn ceremony.

### On the wedding day

On the morning of the wedding, the best man arrives at the groom's place and helps him get ready. He makes sure the groom looks presentable, helps him put on his boutonniere, and offers emotional support.

During the bride ransom, the best man actively participates in contests and games, helping the groom «free» his beloved. He should stock up on small bills and gifts for the bridesmaids in advance.

At the registry office, the witness signs the registration book, officially confirming the conclusion of the marriage. This is one of the most solemn moments, where the role of witnesses is especially important.

## Responsibilities of the witness

### Wedding preparation

The witness has an equally important role. The duties of the witness include:

-   Organizing a bachelorette party for the bride
-   Help choosing a wedding dress and accessories
-   Bridesmaid coordination
-   Preparing to Say Goodbye to the Single Life

### On the day of the celebration

A bridesmaid performs many tasks. She helps the bride with her makeup and hair, keeps an eye on her appearance throughout the day, and adjusts her dress and veil.

During the ransom, the bridesmaid organizes contests for the groom and his friends, ensuring that «the bride isn't stolen» too easily. She actively participates in creating a fun atmosphere.

## Witnesses: Working as a Team

![Wedding Witness. Agence Chance](/media/202508-russkie-na-mavrikii-11-1280.webp)

The bridesmaids' task is to work as a unified team to ensure a perfect wedding day. They need to coordinate their efforts, make sure everything runs according to plan, and be prepared for unexpected situations.

During the wedding ceremony, the witnesses stand next to the newlyweds, offering emotional support. After registering the marriage at the civil registry office, they participate in a photo shoot and accompany the newlyweds to the banquet venue.

## What should a witness do during a banquet

At the banquet, the witnesses act as the toastmaster's main assistants. The witness must:

-   A toast to the newlyweds
-   Organize guests to participate in competitions
-   Make sure the newlyweds' glasses are always full.
-   Assist in coordinating the wedding procession
-   Maintain a fun atmosphere during the celebration

## Witness appearance requirements

Witnesses should look festive, but not overshadow the newlyweds. The best man typically wears a classic suit that complements the groom's attire. A boutonniere is pinned to the lapel.

The witness is choosing an elegant dress, avoiding white (which is the bride's color) and overly bright shades. It's important to coordinate the outfit with the bride in advance.

## Modern trends and signs

Nowadays, the role of witnesses has evolved. If previously they had to be people of the opposite sex to the newlyweds, now it is not mandatory. Witnesses can be both men and women; the main thing is their closeness to the bride and groom.

There are many superstitions associated with witnesses. It is believed that witnesses should not be divorced so as not to «pass on» bad luck to the young couple. Although this is a superstition, many couples still adhere to this tradition.

## Participation in a wedding ceremony

![Wedding Witness. Agence Chance](/media/202508-russkie-na-mavrikii-12-1280.webp)

If newlyweds plan a church wedding after registering with the Civil Registry Office (ZAGS), which is considered the official place of marriage, the witnesses play an important role in the church ceremony as well. They hold the crowns over the heads of the bride and groom during the wedding, which is a great honor and responsibility.

## Post-wedding duties

The work of the maid of honor and best man doesn't end when the wedding reception is over. They can help the newlyweds with cleanup, collecting gifts, and returning rented items. The maid of honor and best man should be prepared that their support may also be needed after the celebration.

## Tips for Future Bridesmaids

Those invited as witnesses should:

-   Discuss all details and expectations with the newlyweds in advance.
-   Prepare physically and emotionally for a long day
-   Review the wedding program and your responsibilities.
-   Be prepared for the unexpected and maintain a positive attitude
-   Remember that the main thing is to support the young couple on their special day

Witnesses should be people whom the bride and groom completely trust. They are responsible for organizing many aspects of the celebration, so it is important to approach this role seriously and responsibly.
