When you connect to our website, AGENCE CHANCE may install various cookies on your device. Some cookies require your prior consent and will only be placed if you have previously accepted them. To ensure transparency regarding the personal data we collect and process, we explain through this cookie policy what a cookie is, what types of cookies are used, for what purpose, who the recipients of the data collected are, and what options you have to manage these cookies.

## Definitions

A cookie is a small text file stored on the hard drive of the user's device (computer, tablet, smartphone, internet-connected game console, etc.) when browsing a website, viewing an advertisement, reading an email, or installing or using software or a mobile application, regardless of the type of device used. When you connect to a website or browse a mobile application, a cookie may be transmitted to your browser by the website or application you are visiting. Your web browser will keep it for a certain period and send it back to the web server with each new connection.

## Our commitments

AGENCE CHANCE uses the same open-source solution used by the CNIL, tarteaucitron.js, to manage the collection of consents or refusals for the deposit or reading of cookies and to respect your right to withdraw consent. This solution is hosted on our Host's servers and is regularly updated by our services.  
Concerned about respecting your privacy, AGENCE CHANCE wants to reassure you: simply visiting our website (homepage or directly on another page of the site from a search engine, for example) and continuing or not continuing your navigation does not constitute acceptance and does not lead to the installation of Cookies requiring your prior consent. As long as you have not accepted them, no cookie requiring prior consent will be placed or read on your device. Refusing to give your consent or not making a choice does not prevent access to our website.

## 2\. What is the duration of my choice (consent or refusal)?

We are aware that consent to «be tracked» may be forgotten by individuals who have given it at a given moment. For this reason, your choice upon installation of cookies is valid for 6 months after the first deposit on your device. At the end of this period, your choice will be requested again. Furthermore, you can change your mind at any time by visiting the Cookie Management page located at the bottom of each page of our website.

## 3\. What cookies are used?

Different types of cookies are placed on the site:

-   **Technical cookies necessary for the proper functioning of the site**  
    These cookies are strictly necessary for the functioning of the Site and/or for the provision of a service expressly requested by the user. They cannot be deactivated by default. The data collected by these cookies are kept for the duration of your session.
-   **Other cookies:**  
    The legal basis for all the following cookies is your prior consent. During your first visit to the Site, a banner informs you of the presence of these cookies and invites you to indicate your choice. They are only deposited if you accept them.
-   **Audience measurement cookies**  
    Audience measurement cookies allow AGENCE CHANCE to understand how visitors access our website and to obtain statistics related to their browsing journey in order to optimize its ergonomics, content, etc.
-   **Advertising cookies**  
    Advertising cookies allow AGENCE CHANCE to display the most relevant ads tailored to your interests based on your browsing history. The data collected by these cookies is kept for 12 months.
-   **Social networks and video**  
    The cookies placed via social media buttons are intended to allow site users to easily share content and improve user-friendliness.  
    These social media buttons use cookies directly deposited by the aforementioned third-party service providers to track internet users« browsing habits, whether you are a user of these platforms or not, and whether you are logged into the social network or not while browsing. The data collected by these cookies also allows for targeted advertising offers on these platforms (See »Advertising Cookies").  
    We inform you that AGENCE CHANCE does not collect any data through these cookies but allows these third parties, through the installation of these features on its Site, to collect data on Site visitors.

## 5\. How to exercise your GDPR rights?

You have the right to withdraw your consent to the deposit and/or reading of cookies subject to your prior consent at any time using our cookie management tool, accessible from each page of the Site. In accordance with and within the limits of applicable legal provisions, you have the right to access your data or to request its erasure. You also have the right to object, the right to rectification, the right to restrict the processing of your data, the right to data portability, and the right to define directives concerning the fate of your personal data after your death (see. [www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr) for more information on your rights).

To exercise one of your rights, send your request to:

-   email: agence.chance.france@gmail.com
