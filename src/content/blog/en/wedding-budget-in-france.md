A wedding is one of the most important and memorable events in everyone's life. Especially if it takes place in such a romantic country as France. However, behind the romance lies serious planning and correct budget calculation. In this article, we will detail the 10 essential and sufficient points for a successful wedding in France, and explain how to properly allocate your wedding budget.

## The main expenses in a wedding budget

### Banquet venue

Start with this—it will set the tone for the entire event. Choosing a venue is a key factor that determines the atmosphere of the entire celebration and accounts for a significant portion of the wedding budget. It’s best to look for what are known as salles de fêtes or salles de mariages—they have all the necessary permits and are equipped with soundproofing and the right facilities. In Paris, venues start at 5,000 euros per evening. If you travel about an hour by car from the capital, you’ll find venues that are up to 30% cheaper.

French wedding venues offer a variety of options, from historic castles and chateaux to modern banquet halls with panoramic views. When choosing a venue, it's important to consider not only the rental cost but also accessibility for guests and the seasonality, which can significantly impact the overall wedding budget.

### 2\. Restoration and Food

Wedding venues will most often offer their services or those of their trusted contractors. French cuisine is known for its exquisite taste and variety, making catering one of the most significant parts of the wedding budget. Be sure to arrange a food tasting before confirming: you can always adjust the menu or invite your own caterer. Food prices start from €55 per person in the provinces to €120 in Paris (excluding drinks).

It is worth noting that French catering companies usually offer various service packages that include not only food preparation and catering but also service, which is important to consider when planning a wedding budget. Many services also offer special children's menus and take into account guests' dietary preferences.

### 3\. Drinks

Most venues allow you to bring your own alcohol. We recommend taking advantage of this option; it will be cheaper and allow you to save a significant portion of your wedding budget. Bring all the drinks a few days in advance so that the wine has time to settle and the champagne can be chilled for the celebration.

France is a country of wine, so choosing the right drinks can truly enhance your celebration. Many wineries offer special wedding collections or the possibility to create personalized labels. When calculating the volume of drinks, consider approximately 0.5-0.75 bottles of wine per guest and 1-2 glasses of champagne for toasts.

## Creating Atmosphere: Decor and Entertainment within a Wedding Budget

![Wedding Budget in France - Agence Chance](/media/202505-dizayn-bez-nazvaniya-76-1280.webp)

### 4\. Decoration

Ask what decor is included in the venue rental. Often, it's nothing but bare tables and chairs. If you order catering, white tablecloths and all necessary cutlery, dishes, and napkins are usually included in the package. Sometimes, flowers for the center of the tables are offered: artificial, and rarely fresh. All other decor: cocktail table decorations, a floral arch, ceiling or terrace decoration – that's all the decorator's job. The cost of their services starts from €2000.

Decor holds a special place in a wedding budget as it creates the visual aesthetic for the entire celebration. French-style wedding decor typically combines elegance and romance. To reduce costs, you can focus on a few key decorative elements that will make the biggest impact: a floral arch for the ceremony, centerpieces for the tables, and photo booth decor.

### 5\. DJ and Equipment

Light, sound, mixers, microphones, special effects — these are all non-obvious things that are absolutely necessary for your celebration to create the right atmosphere. Sometimes there's a minimum available at the venue. Sometimes the DJ has all this wealth. But often, all of this has to be rented. The cost of this service starts from €1000.

Good technical equipment is the key to a successful celebration, and this needs to be factored into the wedding budget. Modern DJs in France typically offer not just musical accompaniment, but also lighting shows, operation of smoke machines, and other special effects. When choosing a DJ, pay attention to their repertoire and their willingness to work with a multilingual audience if you will have guests from different countries.

### Musicians

Live music always adds soul to an event and can become an unforgettable part of the celebration, enhancing the wedding budget with an investment in emotions. A single musician will cost you from €300 for a short performance.

French weddings often feature live music during the cocktail hour or ceremony. This could be a string quartet, a jazz ensemble, or even an accordionist to create a Parisian atmosphere. The cost of musical accompaniment depends on the number of musicians, the duration of their performance, and their popularity.

### 7\. Animator or host

At French weddings, this role isn't the most sought-after, as guests often set the tone for the event themselves. And with a good DJ and musicians, the atmosphere creates itself. However, for multilingual weddings, we recommend considering this option. A host will be able to speak in different languages, as well as create the necessary bridge among a diverse audience, adding entertainment to the program that will relax and unite guests. Approximately €1500.

When planning an international wedding, this budget item may be particularly relevant. A professional MC (Master of Ceremonies) not only coordinates the evening's program but also helps overcome language barriers, making the celebration comfortable for all guests. Many French MCs have experience with international weddings and can offer tailored entertainment.

## Capturing the moment and wedding budget logistics

![Wedding Budget in France - Agence Chance](/media/202505-dizayn-bez-nazvaniya-77-1280.webp)

### 8\. Photographer and Videographer

Absolutely essential people at the celebration. A wedding doesn't happen every day, and capturing these unforgettable moments with quality is the foundation of good memories for years to come. Due to the responsibility of the task, wedding photo and video shooting costs more than usual. Feel free to budget €2500 - €3000 for photo and video.

France, with its historical monuments, picturesque streets, and natural beauty, offers endless opportunities for magnificent wedding photography. Professional photo and video shoots are an investment in memories, which is why many couples allocate a significant portion of their wedding budget to this item. When choosing a photographer, pay attention not only to their portfolio, but also to their knowledge of the area and their ability to work with natural light.

### 9\. Transportation

A beautiful motorcade, with at least one car for the newlyweds, decorated with flowers – a lovely addition to your celebration and a great prop for your photoshoot. Rental for a few hours starts at €300.

Wedding budget transportation costs include not only the wedding car for the newlyweds but also guest transportation between the ceremony, reception, and accommodation. In France, you can choose from classic limousines to vintage cars characteristic of French culture, such as the Citroën 2CV or Renault 4L. To create an atmosphere of French romance, some couples may even opt for horse-drawn carriages in historic locations.

### 10\. Accommodation

When you move away from Paris, you need to consider accommodation for guests (it could be at your expense or theirs – that depends on your guests). In the provinces, the price for a double room is around €100. Sometimes you may need to arrange a bus for guests.

Accommodation costs can represent a significant portion of a wedding budget, especially if you plan to cover these expenses for your guests. Many French wedding venues offer on-site accommodation or partner with nearby hotels, allowing for group discounts. For your guests' comfort, it's advisable to book a certain number of rooms in advance with the option to cancel without penalty.

## Additional expenses and wedding budget planning

The bride's hair and makeup (approximately €300), as well as the bride's and groom's outfits, will be left out. These personal expenses must also be included in the overall wedding budget. French stylists are known for their craftsmanship, and many brides prefer to book a trial makeup and hairstyle a few weeks before the wedding.

You can add "cherry on top" elements to the cake, such as the newlyweds' dance to open the ball, a photo booth to entertain guests, releasing glowing balloons into the sky, and other pleasant (and costly :)) little things. Here, your imagination is limitless and is only constrained by legal norms (some things, such as aerial animation, need to be coordinated with the mayor's office).

In general, any wedding, big or small, will cost you from €15,000. To not strain yourself or hold back, budget €30,000 for a middle-up level wedding for 100 people.

## How to optimize a wedding budget?

![Wedding Budget in France - Agence Chance](/media/202505-dizayn-bez-nazvaniya-78-1280.webp)

What can be saved on?

On a package deal. If you book your wedding «all-in-one,» it saves you a colossal amount of time and money: the team is coordinated, the quality is proven, and bargaining is possible.

A wedding planner is here to help you with exactly that. Through their contacts, they can find you quality venues, negotiate prices with vendors, and create a program so that it's clear what you need and what you can give up without issues.

Hiring a wedding planner may seem like an extra expense, but it ultimately helps optimize your wedding budget thanks to their professional approach and established relationships with vendors. Wedding planners in France typically charge around 10–15% of the total budget or a flat fee, depending on the scope of work.

### Seasonal Factors in Wedding Budget Planning

The time of year you choose can significantly affect your wedding budget. The peak wedding season in France runs from May through September, when prices are at their highest. By choosing the off-season (March–April or October–November), you can save up to 30% on venue rentals and vendor services. Winter weddings, while less popular, can be incredibly romantic and significantly more affordable.

### The official part in the wedding budget

If you plan to have not only a festive but also a legal ceremony in France, you must factor in additional expenses. For foreigners, the process can be complex and may require document translations, legalization, and even legal assistance. An alternative could be a symbolic ceremony in France after an official registration in your home country, which would significantly simplify organization and reduce costs.

## Conclusion: Smart wedding budget planning

Planning a wedding in France is an exciting process that requires careful attention to budget and thorough organization. Setting the right priorities will help create an unforgettable celebration without unnecessary financial stress.

Remember that your wedding budget should align with your financial capabilities and priorities. Determine what's most important to you – the venue, catering, photography, or entertainment program – and allocate the larger portion of your budget to these items, while saving on less significant elements.

France offers an endless array of possibilities for a wedding of any size and style. Smart wedding budget planning will allow you to enjoy all the beauty and romance of this amazing country, creating an event that will be remembered for a lifetime.
