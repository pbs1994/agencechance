## What do wedding anniversaries symbolize?

Wedding anniversaries by year have a deep symbolic meaning, reflecting the evolution of the relationship between husband and wife. Each year of marriage is compared to a certain material that symbolizes the strength and character of the marital bond at this stage of family life.

The tradition of naming wedding anniversaries with specific names originated in Germany, but quickly spread throughout Europe, including France. This symbolism helps couples realize how their relationship grows and develops with each year they spend together.

Anniversary names reflect the journey from a fragile initial relationship to a strong and lasting bond. The early years symbolize the tenderness and delicacy of a young marriage, while later anniversaries represent the strength and value of a time-tested relationship.

## The first years of marriage: from fragility to strength

### Paper Anniversary (1 year)

A married couple celebrates the first year of their life together with a paper wedding. Paper symbolizes the purity and newness of relationships, as well as their fragility. Just as a sheet of paper is easily crumpled and torn, young relationships require careful handling and mutual respect.

It is customary to celebrate the anniversary with a romantic dinner, where spouses can write letters to each other with declarations of love and plans for the future. In France, it is popular to give books of poetry or create a photo album with memories of the first year of marriage.

### Chintz anniversary (2 years)

The second anniversary is called the chintz or cotton wedding. Cotton is stronger than paper, which symbolizes the strengthening of family ties. The spouses have already gotten to know each other better, learned to find compromises and build a common household.

Gifts for this anniversary are usually practical and related to home comfort: bed linens, towels, home textiles. This emphasizes the importance of creating a common family nest.

### Leather Jubilee (3 years)

![Wedding Anniversaries. Agence Chance](/media/202507-dizayn-bez-nazvaniya-3-1280.webp)

For the third year of their marriage, the couple is celebrating their leather wedding. Leather symbolizes flexibility and strength at the same time. By this time, the couple has learned to adapt to each other's peculiarities while maintaining the strength of the relationship.

Traditional gifts include leather accessories, handbags, purses or even a trip together to symbolize the willingness to embark on new adventures together.

### Linen Jubilee (4 years)

The fourth wedding anniversary is called the linen anniversary. Flax is a symbol of purity and naturalness. Spouses by this time have created stable family traditions and learned to be natural with each other.

Celebrations often include updating the home interior with linen fabrics, symbolizing renewal and relationship growth.

## Transition years: strengthening family ties

### Wooden anniversary (5 years)

Five years of marriage is celebrated with a wooden wedding. The tree symbolizes growth, stability and vitality. As a tree takes deep roots, family relationships become more stable and strong by this time.

Traditionally, spouses plant a tree in their garden or give each other wooden objects. In France, it is popular to organize picnics in nature, emphasizing the connection to the natural roots of the relationship.

### Cast-iron wedding (6 years)

The sixth year of life together is symbolized by cast iron, a material that is durable but still requires maintenance. The spouses have learned to cope with difficulties, but continue to work on strengthening their relationship.

Gifts are usually practical and durable, symbolizing the reliability and stability of family life.

### Copper Jubilee (7 years)

The seventh anniversary is called the copper wedding. Copper is a noble metal that symbolizes the beauty of relationships and their ability to «polish» and improve. Seven years of life together have shown that the couple can overcome obstacles and become stronger.

Traditional gifts include copper jewelry or decorative items, and the celebration often takes place in a warm family atmosphere.

## Significant milestones in family life

### Tin Jubilee (8 years)

The eighth year of marriage is celebrated with a pewter wedding. Pewter symbolizes flexibility and adaptability. Spouses by this time have gone through many trials and learned to adapt to changes in life.

### Porcelain anniversary (9 years)

The ninth wedding anniversary is called the porcelain wedding anniversary. Porcelain is beautiful, but fragile, which reminds the spouses of the need to take care of family relationships. Nine years of married life have shown that the beauty of a relationship requires constant attention and care.

Celebrations often include an elegant dinner using the finest tableware, symbolizing the special value of the moment.

### Pink wedding (10 years)

![Wedding Anniversaries. Agence Chance](/media/202507-dizayn-bez-nazvaniya-2-1280.webp)

The tenth year of life together is celebrated with a rose wedding. The rose symbolizes love, beauty and tenderness. Ten years of marriage have shown that the spouses' feelings have not only survived, but also blossomed like a beautiful rose.

It is a significant milestone that is celebrated with a special celebration, inviting close friends and relatives. Pink flowers, pink wine and a romantic atmosphere create an unforgettable celebration.

## Silver and gold milestones

### Silver wedding anniversary (25 years)

Twenty-five years of life together is a silver wedding, one of the most important and solemn anniversaries in family life. Silver symbolizes purity, nobility and the true value of relationships that have passed the test of time. A quarter of a century of marriage has shown that the spouses have created a strong union capable of withstanding all the trials of life.

By this time, husband and wife have gone through many stages: the young years of passionate love, the period of harmonization of characters, perhaps the birth and upbringing of children, career ups and downs. Twenty-five years of married life means that the spouses have learned not only to love, but also to understand each other at the deepest level.

Celebrating a silver wedding is often a big family event with the renewal of marriage vows. Many couples organize a grand ceremony, inviting everyone who witnessed their wedding day. Traditional gifts include silver jewelry, cutlery, picture frames and other décor items made of the noble metal.

In France, it is customary to give silver rings as a symbol of renewal of the marriage union. Franco-Russian couples can combine traditions by arranging both a French elegant dinner and a Russian feast with toasts and songs.

### Pearl wedding (30 years)

Thirty years of marriage are celebrated with a pearl wedding - one of the most beautiful and symbolic anniversaries. Pearls symbolize wisdom, patience and beauty that is formed gradually, layer by layer. Just as a pearl is created in a shell over years, transforming a grain of sand into a jewel, so a strong family relationship is formed over decades, turning everyday moments into treasures of memory.

Thirty years of life together is a time when the spouses have reached a special harmony. They have learned to appreciate not only great joys, but also small daily manifestations of love and care. Pearl wedding symbolizes the maturity of relationships, when each conflict and its resolution added a new «layer» to the strength of the marriage.

Pearl wedding celebrations are often held with family and closest friends. Traditional gifts include pearl jewelry, which symbolizes the beauty and rarity of such a long, happy marriage. Many couples become grandparents by this time, which adds a special meaning to the celebration.

### Coral wedding anniversary (35 years)

Thirty-five years of life together is called a coral wedding. Coral is an amazing sea creature that grows slowly but creates strong and beautiful structures. Like coral reefs, family relations by this time have become a real foundation on which the lives of not only the spouses, but also their children and grandchildren are built.

Coral wedding symbolizes the firmness and longevity of family bonds. The couple has lived together for more than three decades, experiencing many changes in themselves and the world around them. They have seen times, technology and society change, but their love and devotion to each other has remained unchanged.

Coral wedding celebrations often include trips to the sea, symbolizing the depth and boundlessness of feelings. Gifts are traditionally associated with nautical themes or have the reddish pink color characteristic of coral. This is a time for reflecting on the path traveled and planning future joint projects.

### Ruby wedding anniversary (40 years)

Forty years of marriage are celebrated with a ruby wedding - one of the brightest and most significant anniversaries. Ruby is a precious stone of royal dignity, symbolizing passion, love and unshakeable durability. Four decades of life together have shown that the love of the spouses has not faded away, but has become even deeper and more valuable, like a ruby, which only becomes more beautiful over the years.

Ruby wedding celebrations are often large family gatherings where several generations come together. Traditional gifts include ruby jewelry, red roses, and decor items in red tones. Many couples update their wedding rings by this time, adding ruby inlays to them.

In French tradition, a ruby wedding is often accompanied by a gala dinner with red wine from the places where the couple spent their honeymoon or other memorable moments. Russian traditions add the warmth of a family feast with memories and well-wishes.

### Golden wedding anniversary (50 years)

![Wedding Anniversaries. Agence Chance](/media/202507-dizayn-bez-nazvaniya-1-1280.webp)

Fifty years of life together is the golden wedding, the most honored and solemn anniversary in family life. Gold symbolizes the highest value, permanence and nobility. Half a century of marriage is a real feat of love and devotion, worthy of admiration and deep respect.

Traditional gifts for a golden wedding include gold jewelry, watches, art objects. But the most valuable gift is the presence of all generations of the family - children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, who are a living testimony to the success and fruitfulness of this marriage.

In France, a golden wedding is often accompanied by a renewal of marriage vows in the same church where the wedding took place half a century ago. Franco-Russian couples can organize a double celebration, combining French elegance with Russian soulfulness and breadth of celebration.

## Rare anniversaries: emerald and beyond

### Emerald wedding anniversary (55 years)

Fifty-five years of marriage are celebrated with an emerald wedding - a rare and special anniversary that few couples reach. Emerald is one of the most valuable gemstones, symbolizing wisdom, harmony and eternal youthfulness of spirit. As an emerald retains its beauty and brilliance through millennia, so the love of spouses who have lived together for more than half a century remains bright and unfading.

Emerald wedding symbolizes the achievement of the highest degree of family harmony. The spouses by this time have gained the wisdom that comes only with years. They have learned not only to accept each other as they are, but also to find beauty in every day of life together. Fifty-five years together means that the couple has experienced not only joys, but also many trials, each of which has made their bond even stronger.

### Diamond wedding anniversary (60 years)

Sixty years of life together is a diamond wedding, one of the rarest and most honored anniversaries. Diamond is the most durable natural material, symbolizing the steadfastness, perfection and eternal beauty of a relationship. Just as a diamond is formed under tremendous pressure in the bowels of the earth, a marriage lasting six decades has hardened in the crucible of life's trials and become truly precious.

By this time, spouses often become patriarchs and matriarchs of large family clans. Children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, and perhaps even great-great-grandchildren gather around them. A diamond wedding becomes a celebration not only for the family, but for the entire community, demonstrating the highest examples of family values.

Celebrating a diamond wedding requires special sensitivity, given the age of the anniversary couple. Traditional gifts include diamond jewelry, crystal pieces, and memorable albums with photos of all the years of life together.

### Iron wedding anniversary (65 years)

Sixty-five years of marriage is called the iron wedding, symbolizing the exceptional strength and resilience of the family union. Iron, the metal that underlies all modern civilization, symbolizes reliability, strength and the ability to withstand any load. Spouses who have achieved an iron wedding demonstrate that their relationship has become the very foundation upon which the lives of several generations of their family have been built.

Iron wedding celebrations are often of a particularly solemn nature. Many couples receive official awards and commendations from government and community organizations by this time. Traditional gifts include iron or steel objects symbolizing the strength of the marriage, as well as flowers and gifts reflecting the long history of the family.

### Graceful wedding (70 years)

Seventy years of life together is a gracious wedding, symbolizing the divine blessing of a long and happy marriage. Only a handful of couples reach such an anniversary, and every couple that celebrates a graceful wedding becomes a legend to their family and community. Seventy years is an era in which the world has changed beyond recognition, but the love of the couple has remained unchanged.

The Grace Wedding is so called because such a long and happy marriage is seen as a special favor of fate. Spouses who have lived together for seven decades are the guardians of family history, a living link between the past, present and future. Their wisdom and life experience become an invaluable legacy for all generations of the family.

The celebration of a graceful wedding is often accompanied by special ceremonies of recognition and thanksgiving. Many religious denominations view such a marriage as a model of Christian union. Gifts for a graceful wedding are often symbolic in nature - it can be a specially written family chronicle, a family tree or a commemorative album with the family history.

### Oak wedding anniversary (80 years)

Eighty years of life together is celebrated with an oak wedding - a symbol of incredible strength, longevity and wisdom. Oak is a tree that can live for over a thousand years, symbolizing constancy and steadfastness. Spouses who have achieved an oak wedding demonstrate the same qualities - their love has become as mighty and eternal as the ancient oak tree.

An oak wedding is an anniversary that defies all notions of the possibilities of human love and devotion. Eighty years together means that the couple has gone through all the stages of life several times, seen the world change, but themselves remained unchanged in their attachment to each other.

### Birch wedding (90 years)

Ninety years of marriage are celebrated with a birch wedding. Birch is a symbol of purity, renewal and vitality. Spouses who have reached this inconceivable anniversary show that their love is capable of constant renewal, like a young birch tree every spring.

### Centennial wedding (100 years)

One hundred years of life together is a centenary wedding, an absolute record of human devotion and love. Such an anniversary is almost unattainable, but it remains a symbol of an ideal marriage to which one can aspire. A hundred-year wedding symbolizes the eternity of love, capable of overcoming any boundaries of time.

## Celebration traditions in France

In France, celebrating wedding anniversaries has its own peculiarities. The French appreciate elegance and refinement, so celebrations are often held in a refined atmosphere. Romantic dinners in restaurants, walks in picturesque places and gifts created with special care are popular.

Franco-Russian couples can combine the traditions of both cultures to create unique celebrations. Russian traditions of hospitality and extensive feasting blend beautifully with French sophistication and attention to detail.
